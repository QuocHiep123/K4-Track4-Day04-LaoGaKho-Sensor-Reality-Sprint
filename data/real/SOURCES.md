# Real-world image sources

These source images are used for an educational, non-commercial camera-health benchmark. Generated corruption variants are derived from these images.

## KITTI road/lane samples

- Files in this repository: `kitti_um_000032.png`, `kitti_umm_000005.png`, `kitti_uu_000010.png`
- Original scene identifiers: `um_000032`, `umm_000005`, `uu_000010`
- Dataset: KITTI Road/Lane Benchmark
- Authors: Andreas Geiger, Philip Lenz, Raquel Urtasun and KITTI contributors
- Sample host and attribution: https://csundergrad.science.uoit.ca/courses/csci3240u/latest/labs/data/kitti-samples/ATTRIBUTION.html
- Official dataset: https://www.cvlibs.net/datasets/kitti/
- License: CC BY-NC-SA 3.0; research/coursework and non-commercial use only
- Citation: A. Geiger, P. Lenz and R. Urtasun, “Are we ready for Autonomous Driving? The KITTI vision benchmark suite,” CVPR, 2012.
